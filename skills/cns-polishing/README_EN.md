# `cns-polishing` Skill

[中文说明](README.md)

Polish, restructure, or translate academic prose into English aligned with **Nature**, **Cell**, or **Science** family journals while preserving meaning and evidence boundaries.

## What To Use It For

- Translate Chinese academic paragraphs into submission-ready English
- Tighten verbose sentences and improve argument flow
- Tune abstract, introduction, results, or discussion for a target CNS journal
- Reduce AI-like phrasing, hype, and overstated causality

## Typical Requests

- “Polish this Results paragraph in Nature style without changing the data.”
- “Cell submission — refine the Abstract and Significance statement.”
- “Translate this Introduction into Science-leaning English.”

## What You Need To Provide

- Source text, target journal (Nature / Cell / Science or a sub-journal), and section
- Facts, data, and citations that must not change
- Desired output: rewrite only, or rewrite plus change notes

## Outputs

- Paste-ready English rewrite (optional bilingual list)
- Change notes and items requiring author confirmation

## Boundaries

- Does not add results, mechanisms, significance claims, or references
- Does not draft full sections from scratch (see `cns-writing`)

## Related Skills

- `cns-writing` — section drafting
- `cns-reviewer` — pre-submission review simulation
- `cns-abstract` — abstract formatting and punctuation
